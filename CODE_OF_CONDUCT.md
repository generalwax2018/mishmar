# Code of conduct

Mishmar serves congregations of every faith. Contributors and users come from many traditions, and many are volunteers.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about code, not about people or their beliefs.
- No harassment, slurs or hateful content toward any religion, ethnicity or group. That is never acceptable here.
- Don't share anything that could help someone harm a congregation: real floor plans, schedules, security gaps or member details.
- Maintainers may remove content and block anyone who breaks these rules.

To report a problem privately, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
